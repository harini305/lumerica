## Section 2

A unique approach to healing that combines CranioSacral Therapy with Kundalini Yoga and Sound Healing.

The session begins with a short Kundalini Yoga Kriya, using movement, breathwork and meditation to bring you closer to yourself, your awareness back into your body and preparing you to relax and heal. The Kriya is then followed by a long Savasana, where you will go into a deep state of meditation, supported by a sound bath, while receiving hands-on CranioSacral adjustments.

Based on the body’s innate ability to heal and restore, CranioSacral Therapy tunes in this fascinating mechanism and supports the nervous system to integrate the healing journey.

This is a powerful workshop for those curious about healing using Kundalini Yoga technology, breathwork, meditation, sound healing & craniosacral therapy. Everyone is welcome!
