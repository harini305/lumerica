## Section 3

[Image] https://lumeriayoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Photo-from-Lumeria-Yoga-Bali-1024x682.jpg

The Shamanic Breathwork ceremony is a powerful transformational journey that inspires individuals to awaken the Shaman within and reconnect with it. Shamanic Breathwork honors and blends the timeless wisdom of ancient traditions with the emerging new paradigm methods of healing and teaching. As old wounds and dysfunctional patterns are released and transformed, individuals begin to regain lost soul parts and remember the magic of who they truly are. Empowerment brings wholeness and healing back into their lives.

The Shamanic Breathwork ceremony begins by establishing a safe and sacred space. Individuals lie down and focus on surrendering to their Shaman Within, calling upon that which is most sacred to them. Guided by the heartbeat of a shamanic drum, participants journey through sacred space, supported by chakra-attuned music for deep transformation. As the individual uses the power of conscious connected breathing to create a natural altered dreamlike state, the ego defenses are released, and the journey unfolds in a variety of magical ways. The breath functions as the rainbow bridge between body, mind, heart, soul and spirit.

To fully grasp the transformative power of Shamanic Breathwork, it must be experienced first-hand – each journey is unique and deeply personal.
