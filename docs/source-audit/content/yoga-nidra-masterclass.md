Embark into a journey of healing and self-discovery.

Join us for a transformative experience to deepen your level of awareness through the powerful ancient practice of Yoga Nidra, and explore the depths of relaxation, restoration, and grounding. The masterclass is addressed to all individuals interested in enhanced meditation, and mindfulness, looking to deepen their understanding and practice of Yoga Nidra.
