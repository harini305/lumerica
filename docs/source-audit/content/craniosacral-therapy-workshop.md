## Section 2

We invite you to explore CranioSacral Therapy (CST) as a powerful healing tool, valuable for restoring and maintaining emotional and physical health and overall well-being.

Our aim is to bring you an easy and useful tool to regulate your nervous system, release emotional/mental traumas and balance varying levels of stress. This introductory session into CranioSacral Therapy will allow you to discover the healing power of gentle touch with theory discussion, hands-on practice and Q&A for a well-rounded experience to help you understand the concept of CST, how it works, and get to integrate its power from both ends, both as a patient and as a healer.
