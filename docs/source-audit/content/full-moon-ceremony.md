## Section 2

Full Moon Ceremony is a sacred and transformative gathering that celebrates the luminous energy of the full moon, inviting reflection, release, and renewal. Join us for an evening of fire intentions, yoga nidra and sound healing.

The evening begins with a powerful fire ceremony, where we cast into the flames all that no longer serves us-signifying the destruction of the old and the welcoming of new beginnings. Following this fiery transformation, participants are gently guided into deep relaxation through yoga nidra, a restorative practice that brings the mind into a state of profound awareness and inner calm through guided meditation. This experience is beautifully complemented by sound healing, where harmonic vibrations and sacred instruments envelop the space, cleansing and harmonizing the body, mind, and spirit.

Together, these rituals create a sacred space for reflection, release, and renewal-illuminated by the radiant glow of the full moon’s mystical energy-fostering both collective growth and personal transformation.

No prior experience is necessary. Everyone is welcome.
