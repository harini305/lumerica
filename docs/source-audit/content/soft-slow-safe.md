Experience Tranquility at the Soft, Slow & Safe Retreat

Join Adam and Lola for a transformative journey into peace and healing. Immerse yourself in a sanctuary designed for the soul, where the gentle rhythms of nature will guide your nervous system towards harmony.

Begin your day in a cozy welcome circle, flowing gracefully into a morning gentle flow that awakens the spirit. Savor a nourishing breakfast crafted to fortify your body, followed by an enlightening workshop that unveils the mysteries of your nervous system.

Through carefully guided practices, explore the art of nervous system regulation, allowing the wisdom of your body to unfold in a safe, nurturing environment. As the day unfolds, surrender to the soothing embrace of Yoga Nidra, cacao, and sound healing, an alchemical blend that harmonizes mind, body, and spirit.

Conclude this magical day with a heartfelt closing circle, connecting deeply with yourself and fellow seekers. This one-day retreat is an invitation to rejuvenate your essence and embrace the softness within. Discover the power of stillness and let your journey toward healing begin.

#### This One Day Retreat is Created For You

This retreat is designed as a sanctuary for anyone longing to soften, slow down, and feel safe in their own body again. Whether you’re navigating stress, craving rest, or curious about deeper nervous system healing – this day is for you:

The Overworked & Overwhelmed – If life feels like it’s been running at full speed, this retreat offers you a much-needed pause. Step out of the noise and into a space where your body can exhale, your mind can rest, and your spirit can recalibrate.

Healing Seekers – If you’ve been on your own journey of recovery-emotional, physical, or spiritual-this retreat gives you grounded tools and supportive practices to help regulate your nervous system and reconnect with inner balance.

Yoga & Meditation Practitioners – Already love yoga or meditation? This retreat expands your practice by weaving in nervous system science, Yoga Nidra, cacao, and sound healing, offering you a fuller, embodied experience of calm and presence.

Wellness Practitioners & Space Holders – If you’re usually the one guiding or supporting others, this retreat is your invitation to receive. Everything is taken care of – you simply arrive, rest, and allow yourself to be held.

- Curious Explorers – If you’ve never attended a retreat before, but feel called to explore gentle practices of self-regulation and inner healing, this retreat provides the perfect, safe entry point.

#### Adam

@yogawithadam

[Link] "@yogawithadam" → https://www.instagram.com/yogawithadam

With over a decade of experience in yoga, sound healing, meditation, and breathwork, Adam is a 500HR+ ERYT. His journey into yoga began during a challenging period in his life, leading him to discover the transformative power of these practices.

Adam is passionate about making yoga accessible to everyone, regardless of their background or experience level. His mission is to empower individuals through mindful movement, breath awareness, and the healing vibrations of sound, fostering a nurturing and uplifting environment for all participants. Through his teachings, Adam aims to create a holistic experience that promotes physical, mental, and emotional well-being, helping others to navigate life’s challenges with resilience and grace.

#### Lola

@lola_malaika

[Link] "@lola_malaika" → https://www.instagram.com/lola_malaika/

Lola is a professional kora player, sound therapist and meditation teacher. Her intuitive search for truth and awakening brought her to live and study in India, Japan, Vietnam, Indonesia and many other amazing places on this planet.

Lola’s work synthesizes ancient and contemporary wisdom of mystical bhakti yoga, gentle yin yoga, buddhist dharma, nada yoga, sound healing, and sacred music and chants of Africa. She follows a tradition of NIRGUNA BHAKTI. Lola also facilitates cacao ceremonies and educates people about therapeutic and spiritual powers of this sacred medicine plant.
