Your gut is more than a digestive organ, it is where you process life. Stress, unresolved emotions, travel fatigue, anxiety, and past experiences can accumulate in the digestive system, affecting not only your physical health but also your emotional clarity and energy levels.

In this immersive workshop, we work with therapeutic magnets placed strategically on the body in a specific configuration known as the Digestive Cross. Biomagnetism uses magnets of opposite polarity to help rebalance the body’s internal pH and support its natural self-regulation processes. By placing magnets on key digestive points, we stimulate energetic balance in the stomach, liver, intestines, and nervous system — areas closely linked to emotional storage and stress response.

This is not just theory. It is an embodied experience.

After the magnet placement, you will be guided into a deep sound healing journey, where therapeutic frequencies help your nervous system shift from survival mode into restoration.

The sound supports emotional release, integration, and a gentle reset at a cellular level.

During this workshop, you will:

~ Receive a guided explanation of how biomagnetism works

~ Experience magnets placed on specific digestive points on your body

~ Safe space while your system rebalances

~ Enter a deep relaxation state through vibration and frequencies.

~ Notice shifts in breath, abdominal tension, emotional lightness, and mental clarity

~ Leave with practical awareness of how your gut and emotions are connected
