## Section 2

If you’re looking for a more meaningful experience with Balinese culture, this workshop takes you into the spiritual and sacred ritual of offerings. You’ll learn the history, significance and art of making Canang Sari, the beautiful offerings seen on temples, sidewalks, statues and just about everywhere in Bali. These beautiful little packets of flowers and sweets are not just for its beauty, it is also about understanding the spiritual significance of these offerings and the rhythms of daily life in Bali.

Join us every Sunday afternoon at Lumeria for a chance to experience the true warmth of Balinese hospitality and gain a deeper appreciation for the culture
