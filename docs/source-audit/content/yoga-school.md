## Section 7

Lumeria Yoga School

#### Program Overview

##### Our Vision

At Lumeria, we vision all beings living, thriving and shining from their hearts. We embrace yoga, healing and connection as a lifestyle, and truth and love as our north star. Our mission is to create a loving, nurturing and inclusive space that inspires all beings to embark on the transformative journey of self-discovery and self-illumination. A space to shine.

##### Our Mission & Promise

Lumeria Yoga School has a very simple mission: to become self-illuminated and shine light on truth and love through the sacred practices of yoga. Our promise is to:

- Create a safe and loving space for everyone to experience the path of yoga.

- Invite teachers, healers, visionaries and wisdom keepers to share their knowledge with integrity and authenticity.

- Share the tools, techniques and traditions upholding the lineages from their origins.

##### The Training

The training is designed to challenge, nurture and transform individuals at every level, regardless of whether you plan to become a yoga teacher. Our 200 Hours Yoga Teacher Training focuses on the Hatha/Vinyasa lineage and style of yoga classes so you’ll be trained and confident to teach upon graduation. There will be a strong focus on breath, postures, sequencing and the embodiment of yoga through philosophy teachings.

## Section 17

200 Hours Yoga Teacher Training

#### The Curriculum

- History of Yoga & Its Historical Context Throughout the Ages

- What is Modern Yoga Today and Where/How Did It Detour

- 8 Limbs of Ashtanga Yoga

- 4 Paths of Yoga

- 5 Major Elements of Yoga

- Major Historical & Modern Lineages of Yoga and their Respective Gurus/Teachers

- History of Yoga with Key Dates and Key Ideas such as the Vedas, Vedanta, Hatha, Colonial and Modern Yoga

- Key Familiarity with Major Yogic Texts (i.e. Vedas, Yoga Sutras, Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads, Hatha Yoga Pradipika)

- Yoga Self-Reflection on How the Philosophy of Yoga Relates to Practice and Class on and off the Mat

- Ethics of Yoga including Awareness of the Yoga Sutras and Similar Yogic Ethical Precepts

- Ethics of Yoga as a Professional Yoga Teacher

- Ethics of Yoga as a Business in the Yoga Industry

- Sun Salutation A, B and Classical Sun Salutation

- Foundational & More Advanced Postures including Sitting, Standing, Inversions, Backbends and Arm Balances Categories

- Proper & Improper Sequencing as it relates to Asanas, Energetics, Anatomical Alignment and more

- Asana Sequencing for Various Students & Environments, i.e. Age, Gender, Country, etc.

- Asana Sequencing with Principles of Entry and Exit by Postures

- Common Misunderstandings & Corrections of Asanas in Sequencing

- Hatha/Vinyasa Sequencing with Principles of Inhalations & Exhalations

- Foundational Sequencing for Warming Up, Sequencing, Cooling Down and Savasana

- Historical Context of Pranayama & Subtle Body

- Effects of Pranayama on Anatomy & Subtle Body

- Major Pranayama Techniques for Stand-Alone Practice or Incorporation into a Yoga Class

- Complete Sequencing of Pranayama with Alternatives, Adaptations and Effects on Subtle Body

- Pranayama Techniques for Passive & Active Nervous System Regulation and Effects on Subtle Body

- Pranayama Techniques and Relationship to Subtle Body including Koshas, Chakras, Nadis, Prana Vayus and more

- Introduction to Ayurveda – Doshas, Gunas and Lifestyle

- Key Postures for Meditation

- Major Meditation Methods by Lineage

- Key Meditation Techniques and Effects on Subtle Body

- Energetic Practice for Meditation including Chanting, Mantras, Mudras, Bandhas and more

- How to Create a Meditation Practice at Home and Incorporate it into a Yoga Class

- 4 Main Systems: Skeletal, Muscular, Respiratory and Nervous Systems

- Major Bones, Types of Joints Involved, Type of Joint Movement in Asana and Yoga Class

- Major Muscles Involved in Asanas and their Contraindications

- Nervous System Regulation including the “Fight, Flight, Freeze” Stress Response and Overall Mind-Body Connection

- Respiratory System including Muscles that Affect Breathing, Involuntary vs Voluntary Breath and How Air Enters and Leaves the Body

- Bio-mechanics of Safe Movement as it pertains to Balancing, Stretching, Awareness and Physical Limitations

- Bio-mechanics in Asanas as it relates to Anatomy with Contraindications, Misalignment and Adaptations

- Learn, practice and teach pranayama (breathwork) techniques, as a stand-alone practice and/or how to incorporate them into a yoga class or your own self-practice

- Learn about body & hand seals (mudras), how they affect our body, mind and emotions, and how to sequence mudras into a yoga class or your own self-practice

- Learn, understand and practice body locks (bandhas) for pranayama and energetic control of the body, breath and mind

- Learn, understand and practice mantras to direct the mind and activate specific chakra centers in the body

- Learn, understand and practice meditation techniques, and how to sequence meditation into a yoga class or your own self-practice

- Principles of Asana & Sequencing including Sitting, Standing, Inversions, Backbends and more

- Principles of Asana Adjustments including Touch, Voice Command and more

- Principles of Class Instruction including Pacing, Cueing, Class Management, Student Management, Environment and more

- Principles for Creating, Building, Sequencing and Teaching

- Professional Development: Yoga-related professional organizations, including the Yoga Alliance Credentialing process

- Professional Development for General Professionalism, including Timeliness, Consistency, Cleanliness and more

- Professional Development for a Successful Yoga Career including Business Plan, Branding, Marketing, Promotions and more

- Administrative Tasks as a Professional Yoga Teacher including Legal Disclaimers, Liability Insurance, Waivers, Booking, Invoicing and more

##### Training Participation Policy

###### Payment Terms

After we receive your enrollment application, we will send you an email with booking details. A non-refundable IDR 9,000,000 (approx. USD $500) deposit or full payment is required to reserve and confirm your spot. Shared & Private Resident Packages are assigned on a first come, first served basis. All payments must be settled by 1 month prior to the training start date.

###### Attendance & Commitment

This training requires a high level of commitment and 200 Hours of completion must be fulfilled to graduate and receive the Certificate of Completion. All scheduled sessions must be attended in full. Repeated absences, late arrivals or early departures will affect final evaluation and may impact certification eligibility. We understand that unforeseen circumstances and emergencies may arise beyond your control which may disrupt attendance; please notify the Lead Trainer immediately.

###### Phone Policy

No phones and no filming allowed during morning sadhana or any classes/sessions. Phones may be used for learning and training purposes, at discretion, such as translating terms, Google Translate or writing notes for studying. This training is an invitation to disconnect from the screen and reconnect to yourself, classmates and teachers.

###### Requirements For Certification

Certificate of Completion will be based on: 1) attendance and punctuality 2) completion of assignments & homework 3) professionalism and contribution to the group environment and 4) passing the written final exam and practicum(s). Students who do not meet these requirements may not receive certification but may be given additional opportunities outside of the training to make up for lost hours and/or unmet requirements, to be evaluated on a case by case basis. This is a professional training and all participants are evaluated equally, according to the requirements set by Yoga Alliance, and with respect to the dedication and commitment by all.
