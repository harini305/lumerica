#### Class Description

##### Ashtanga-Inspired

Based on the principles of Ashtanga-Vinyasa Yoga, this dynamic class will begin with Sun Salutations followed by energetic sequencing of postures & breath. Very similar to a Vinyasa Flow & Power Yoga class, be ready to flow, sweat and breath!

##### Bhakti Yoga

A heart-centered class focusing on devotion to your authentic Self, this practice will begin with some Breathwork to open energy centers, followed by postures to build strength and balance, ending with a restorative savasana. Expect to feel more centered, connected and grounded in pure devotion to the divine with yourself and all beings.

##### Breathwork & Sound Healing

##### Chakra Balancing & Sound Healing

A holistic experience designed to harmonize the body’s energy centers (chakras) using the power of sound as participants are guided through deep relaxation using various instruments such as crystal bowls, shamanic drum and chimes, use to produce healing vibrations. These sound frequencies resonate with each 7 main chakras, clearing blockages, restoring balance and promote physical, emotionsl and spiritual well-being. This session may also include guided meditation & breathwork to further deepen the healing process. This session is suitable for anyone looking to restore balance, release emotions, reduce stress, feel more inner peace and promote overall holistic healing.

##### Gentle Flow

##### Gong Sound Healing

##### Hatha Flow

Hatha Flow is great practice to start every morning. We will practice the traditional style of Surya Namaskara (Sun Salutation) followed by basic, gentle yoga poses connecting breath to movement in a slow and controlled manner. This class is meant to create vitality, immunity and overall improved health in the cardiovascular, muscular and nervous systems and is suitable for all levels, especially beginners who are new to yoga.

##### Healing Sound Massage

Healing Sound Massage is a soothing meditative experience designed to release stagnant energy and promote deep relaxation and inner peace. In this session, participants will be guided through a combination of meditation techniques, energy work and sacred sound. These techniques will help release trapped energy stored within the body through the strategic use of Tibetan Singing Bowls that will be placed on specific areas of the body, targeting those in need of healing and balance. The sound waves of the Tibetan Bowls will be directed into the body, massaging the cells and releasing tension. The bowls, made from various metals, produce a range of frequencies that have a calming effect on the brain, leading to a meditative state. This shift in brain frequency will facilitate deep relaxation, slowing the heart rate down and allowing the body to rejuvenate.

##### Inside Flow

Inside Flow is a dynamic and creative style of Vinyasa yoga where movement, breath, and music become one. Each class takes you on a journey of building a choreographed sequence, step by step, in harmony with the rhythm of the music. It’s more than a physical practice – it’s an emotional experience that invites you to express yourself fully. This is yoga like you’ve never felt before: creative, fluid, and deeply connective.

##### Myofascial Yin

Myofascial Yin blends the restorative practice of Yin yoga with targeted myofascial release, working directly with the fascia (connective tissue) by using props & myofascial balls with long-held passive poses (typically 3-5 minutes each). Through gentle, sustained pressure, this class aims to release deeply held tension, restore mobility and ease patterns of tightness that stretching alone doesn’t reach. Postures are sequenced along the body’s connective tissue lines and, where relevant, follow Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) meridian pathways, supporting both physical release and a settling of the nervous system. No flexibility or experience is required — this is a practice of patience and presence, ideal for anyone carrying tension from sitting, training, or simply daily life. Expect to leave feeling loosened, spacious, and calm.

##### Power Yoga

##### Qi Gong

##### Restorative Yoga & Nidra

Wind down your day with a soothing combination of restorative yoga & yoga nidra. We will begin with gentle stretches to release stress in the mind & body, moving slowly through postures with support of props to prepare for a guided yoga nidra meditation. Feel tension slowly melt away and leave feeling completely relaxed, restored and more balanced in this soft & subtle yoga & meditation class.

##### Sound Healing

##### Vinyasa Flow

Vinyasa Flow is a dynamic, energetically and physically demanding class that incorporates breath, body, movement, all set to a fun music playlist. By connecting breath to movement in this class, you’ll deepen your ujjayi breath, learn creative sequencing, practice getting into “peak” poses and feel simply amazing after a long savasana. Be ready to flow, sweat and breath!

##### Yin Yang Yoga

Yin Yang Yoga blends two styles of yoga into one practice – bringing together the benefits of passively holding yoga poses with more dynamic sequences and standing postures. Yin and Yang are the Taoist concepts which describe the two relative qualities present in everything. In this practice, we’ll bring equilibrium to both sides, creating balance between stronger, more physical poses directly followed by softer, longer-held poses. Open for all levels.

##### Yin Yoga

Yin Yoga is a slow-paced style of yoga as exercise, incorporating principles of traditional Chinese medicine with poses that are held for longer periods of time than in other styles, typically staying for 3-5 minutes in each pose. The sequences of postures are meant to stimulate the channels of the subtle body known as meridians in Chinese medicine. Yin Yoga poses apply moderate stress to the connective tissues of the body—the tendons, fascia and ligaments—with the aim of increasing circulation in the joints and improving flexibility.
