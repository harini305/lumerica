## Section 2

Your voice is more than sound, it is a healing vibration of your innate truth, has the power to release stagnant energy and emotional tension, a bridge between your heart and mind, and a bridge to the spirit of nature. This immersive journey invites you to reconnect with your authentic expression, awakening the melodies that live within your body, your heart, and your spirit.

In this space, you’ll explore intuitive singing, release blockages in the throat through shamanic vibration techniques that unlocks the chakras, and rediscover the freedom and playfulness of your voice. Through somatic movement, breath practices, and gentle play-based exploration, you’ll deepen your self-trust and expand your capacity for expression. This is a space to soften, to release, to renew and to plant seeds of empowerment to free expression.

Together, we will move through layers of emotion and energy, letting go of old patterns and opening to new possibilities. Guided by the nurturing essence of feminine energy, this experience becomes a ceremony of remembrance, where your voice, your body, and your heart are honored as art.

The journey begins with a grounding sound healing meditation tuned to 432 Hz, harmonizing body and mind. From there, we enter a heart-centered sharing circle, fostering connection, authenticity, and belonging. Through guided vocal activation and ancestral sounds, you’ll explore your unique spirit melody and awaken the voice through depth, play and embodiment.

Movement practices will support the release of stagnant energy, inviting freedom and authenticity into the body. To close, you’ll be held in a deeply nourishing sound healing immersion, featuring channeled vocals, handpan, crystal and Tibetan bowls, harmonium, flute and nature-inspired sounds. This final integration invites you into a state of joy, clarity, and expanded awareness, where your intentions are infused with freedom and possibility.

Join us for an evening of connection, expression, and transformation, where your voice becomes a celebration of your essence.
