##### Hot Sauna

Saunas and sweat therapy have been used by ancient cultures from all over the world for thousands of years, from Turkish baths and Native American sweat lodges to Russian banyas and Finnish sweat rooms. The long-lasting health benefits are plenty and have been proven to improve overall cardiovascular health, speed up muscle recovery times, decrease chronic low back pain, improve sleep, lower depression symptoms, support excretion of toxins and heavy metals and prevent fewer colds and viral infections.

[Image] https://lumeriayoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Lumeria-Maria-_-Abz-01770-760x760.jpg

##### Steam Room

Whilst saunas and steams have similar benefits due to both their detoxifying and relaxing nature, the steam room experience is beloved by many yogis and wellness enthusiasts due to it’s cleansing properties including lowering blood pressure, improving blood flow to sore muscles, clearing congestion and mucus in the body, improving skin health and overall skin glow, lowering joint stiffness and lingering peaceful effects.

[Image] https://lumeriayoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Lumeria-Maria-_-Abz-01705-760x760.jpg

##### Cold Plunge​

The use of cold therapy is not a new trend where earliest records go as far back as 5,000 years ago to the Egyptians. In the 17th century physicians such as John Floyer proclaimed the health benefits of bathing in cold water and now sports athletes, wellness coaches, trainers, yogis and others alike are making cold baths an everyday lifestyle due to the mind-body benefits including overall boost in energy levels and immune system, strengthening the nervous system, reducing stress, reducing inflammation, improving metabolic function, improving deep sleep and speeding up muscle repair times.
