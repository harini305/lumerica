##### What to Expect

Somatic Movement & Intention Setting: We begin our journey with a sharing circle to set intentions, followed by somatic movement to awaken the body’s natural intelligence. Somatics is the practice of listening to and moving with the wisdom of the body rather than overriding it with the mind. Through breath and activation of the heart meridian, you’ll cultivate safety, presence, and space for authentic expression to emerge.

Blindfolded Journey into Feminine Embodiment: You’ll be guided inward to explore your inner landscape through movement, breath, and sound. Blindfolded practice invites freedom from external distractions, allowing intuition to awaken and the body to remember its natural rhythms.

Elemental Embodiment Exploration: Through sound, movement, and breath, we journey with the elements as portals of transformation:

– Ether – Freedom: Expand into spacious awareness, awaken intuition, and connect with the unseen.

– Fire – Dissolving: Burn through resistance and release what no longer serves, opening to renewal.

– Water – Emotions: Flow with your emotional body, honoring it as a source of wisdom and creative energy.

– Wood – Stillness: Root into presence and embody inner strength, resilience, and spacious awareness.

Healing Sound Bath & Musical Journey: You will be immersed in sound healing frequencies tuned to 432Hz, resonating with the Earth’s vibration to support grounding, balance, and deep relaxation. The soundscape will weave through your journey with instruments and voice:

– Intuitive Singing: Soulful vocals and light language to awaken remembrance and intuition.

– Gong: Expansive resonance to reset the nervous system, dissolve blockages, and expand awareness.

– Harmonium: Heart-opening tones to anchor presence, devotion, and emotional release.

– Crystal Bowls: Tuned to the heart chakra and pineal gland, clearing blockages and supporting alignment.

– Shamanic Drum: A grounding pulse that fosters empowerment, embodiment, and presence.

– Handpan in D Minor: Celestial tones that invite reflection, relaxation, and sensitivity.

– Nature Seeds & Shakers: Activating natural rhythms and grounding Kundalini energy.

#### About Julia

[Button] "@awildbreathwork" → https://www.instagram.com/awildbreathwork

Julia Aurora is a Scandinavian ceremonialist, sound therapist, yoga & meditation & breathwork focused on neurocognitive function and nervous system rewire. Rooted in somatics and feminine nature, she guides deep embodiment through breath, sound, somatic movement, and art therapy. Weaving earth’s wisdom with the ethereal frequencies of light language, she invites a homecoming, where intuition awakens and the soul remembers its song.

Julia carries the teachings of shamanicas, offering a space where music becomes medicine channeled from water and trees. Through breath and sacred sound, she alchemizes pain into pleasure, tension into flow, and limitation into liberation. Her classes blend handpan, crystal bowls, shamanic drums, chacapa, and nature seeds—each carrying the voices of the elements, harmonizing body, spirit, and breath into one rhythm.
