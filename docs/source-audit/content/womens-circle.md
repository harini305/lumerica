## Section 2

A sacred space to reawaken the feminine through voice, story, and sisterhood.

Join a sacred gathering space for women to reconnect with their inner voice, their ancestral wisdom, and each other. These circles are rooted in vocal alchemy, elemental ritual, and embodied remembrance — weaving together song, breath, storytelling, silence, and sacred intention. Held in rhythm with the lunar phases, each gathering becomes a portal for deep transformation and connection — to self, to sisterhood, and to the greater cycles of life.

## Section 3

Each circle is dedicated to one of the foundational themes of the feminine journey:

1. Self & Sovereignty (New Moon) — Coming home to your voice, body, and truth. A reclamation of identity, boundaries, and personal power.

2. Relationships & Heart (First Quarter) — Exploring love, intimacy, and sisterhood. Healing wounds of the feminine through conscious connection and shared expression.

3. Ancestry & Lineage (Full Moon) — Honoring those who came before us. We gather in ceremony to remember, release, and bless the stories carried in our bloodlines.

4. Vision & Devotion (Last Quarter) — Sowing seeds for the future. Aligning with your path, sacred service, and the essence you are here to embody and offer.

Each circle includes a combination of:

- Vocal Activation & Sound Alchemy

- Elemental Ritual & Circle Sharing

- Moon-Aligned Themes & Practices

- Movement, Breath & Embodiment

- Water Prayers & Earth-Based Wisdom

#### About Marlia

#### @I_am_MarliaCoeur

Journey alongside Marlia Coeur, a multi-instrumentalist and music producer from Cyprus, dedicated to helping others rediscover their authentic voice. As a Music Academy founder & Certified Vocal Coach, Marlia combines breathwork, vocal guidance and energetic practices to unlock human potential through sound, guiding people to tap into their voice’s true power.

She is touring the world offering live performances and circle-singing workshops at major music festivals, becoming a powerful figure in the world of ceremonial music & music liberation mentorship. Marlia loves to spend hours in the studio recording & producing her own albums (look her up on Spotify), a living proof that a determined independent artist with no label can make it work in the world today! Discover her newest vocal courses online or attend one of her empowering retreats in Bali or Greece. Follow her journey and music @I_am_MarliaCoeur
