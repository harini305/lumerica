#### About Emil

#### emiljohannes_

[Link] "emiljohannes_" → https://www.instagram.com/emiljohannes_/

Emil Johannes is a devoted guitarist, vocalist, songwriter, and sound healer with a heartfelt passion for guiding others into the power and beauty of their own voice. Since 2021, Emil has hosted hundreds of kirtans, singing circles, and music gatherings across Bali, offering spaces where people can connect deeply with themselves, with community, and with the divine through music.

He has shared his music at renowned venues including Alchemy, Sayuri, Udara, Zest, The Mansion, Yoga Barn, Nuanu, and Istana, and has collaborated with celebrated artists and devotees such as Vasudev, Kailash Kokopelli, Sayuri Tanaka, Diana Beauty, Nick Wallacki, Jila Ji, Sky on Guitar ,Kiya Ankara, Yoshiki Horita, Free England Jr and many more.

Emil’s work is rooted in devotion and a clear intention: to open hearts, awaken courage, and inspire others to express themselves freely. He weaves together guitar, voice, and presence to create an atmosphere where vulnerability feels safe and music becomes a pathway to transformation.
