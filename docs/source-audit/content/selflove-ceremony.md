## Section 2

Join Adam and Elli this Valentine’s Day for a Self-Love Ceremony, an invitation to slow down, soften, and return to yourself.

We begin by opening sacred space together, creating a container of presence, safety, and intention. Ceremonial cacao supports heart-opening and connection, then we transition into a nourishing, gentle yoga flow designed to release tension and invite ease into the body.

Through Yoga Nidra, a guided meditation, we go deep into the subconscious mind allowing for the amplification of self-love. During this process, sound healing and hands-on Reiki, energy healing, continue to increase the frequencies and vibrations of your heart.

This ceremony is for everyone, single or partnered, and centers on self-love as a lived experience: feeling supported, grounded, and connected within yourself.

Come as you are. Leave rested, nourished, and full of love.
