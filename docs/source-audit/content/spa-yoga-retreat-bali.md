##### Sauna Aufguss Ritual

Aufguss is a traditional European sauna ceremony where heat, steam, aroma, essential oil therapy, and mindful movement combine to amplify the therapeutic effects of the sauna. In this session, you’ll be guided through a multi-sensory ritual that blends:

- Aromatherapy infusions

- Soundscape meditation

- Gentle stretches and breath patterns

- This combination supports circulation, detoxification, and nervous system release, while creating a deeply meditative atmosphere.

Cold Therapy & Nordic Resilience

You’ll learn practical techniques inspired by Nordic traditions to build cold exposure resilience, supporting hormonal balance, mental clarity, and stress adaptability — skills you can safely integrate into daily life.

Deep Integration & Restoration of your Energetic & Emotional System

The journey continues with:

- Biomagnetism and sound medicine for energetic balance

- SPA aromatherapy rituals

- A grounding savasana with homemade eco-sustainable facemasks, allowing your body to fully absorb the benefits of the day

- The experience concludes with a nourishing community lunch, giving you space to connect, reflect, and integrate.
