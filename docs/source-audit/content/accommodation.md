Welcome to a hidden gem nestled in Bali’s lush greenery. Enjoy the blend of eco-luxury accommodation with wellness experiences designed to rejuvenate your body, mind, and spirit.

Your journey with us goes beyond just a place to sleep. Each eco-luxury room is your private retreat, thoughtfully crafted to offer tranquility and comfort. Our yoga sessions welcome everyone, from beginners to seasoned practitioners, offering a space to connect with yourself through movement, sound healing and meditation. ##### A Hidden Oasis Nestled in Central Ubud, Bali

Located in central Ubud, Lumeria is just minutes away from the area’s top attractions, yet it offers a peaceful retreat from the hustle and bustle. Our sanctuary is enveloped by lush nature, ensuring that while you’re close to everything, you’re also beautifully secluded. It’s the ideal base for both exploration and relaxation, where the vibrancy of Ubud meets the tranquility of the Balinese landscape.

[Image] https://lumeriayoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/In-House-Retreat-2.jpg

##### All-Inclusive Holistic Approach to Life

We embrace a holistic, all-inclusive approach to your well-being, creating a haven where every need is met without ever having to step outside. From the nourishing, locally-sourced meals served at our dining area to the rejuvenating yoga classes and detox area, we’ve curated an environment that caters to every aspect of your health and happiness. Here, amidst the serene beauty of Ubud, everything you require for a fulfilling retreat experience is at your fingertips, allowing you to focus fully on your journey of relaxation and rejuvenation.
