Reflect & Renew: New Years Day Retreat at LUMERIA offers a soulful blend of journaling, cacao, intention setting, and restorative practices designed to help you reconnect with your inner self and set a powerful foundation for the new 2026 year ahead.

Join Adam for a day of gentle reflection, heartfelt connection, and renewal. Throughout the day, you’ll engage in mindful vinyasa and yin yoga sessions to balance movement and stillness, followed by deeply restorative Yoga Nidra meditation and sound healing to promote relaxation and inner peace. Tea Ceremony allows you to ground and connect with this plant and its healing qualities. Guided journaling and intention-setting exercises will help you clarify your goals and embrace fresh perspectives, while the ceremonial cacao invites you to open your heart and deepen your connection for yourself. Sauna and ice bath is included and will be available after the Closing Circle.

This is an opportunity to reset, realign, and embrace the possibilities of 2026 with clarity and calm.

No prior experience is necessary; please arrive 10-15 minutes early to settle in.

No phones will be allowed during this experience.

Bring a journal and pen; showers are available.

A full day where you step out of noise, slow your nervous system, and reconnect with the part of you that knows exactly what you want your next year to look like.

You’ll move, breathe, reflect, and integrate. You’ll work with cacao and tea in ceremony, open your heart through gentle yoga, access deep stillness in Yoga Nidra and sound healing, and leave with clarity you can actually feel in your body.

This is not another “New Year’s resolution” ritual. It’s a guided reset designed to help you release the last cycle, anchor into what truly matters, and enter 2026 grounded, spacious, and aligned

#### Adam

[Link] "@yogawithadam" → https://www.instagram.com/yogawithadam

With over a decade of experience in yoga, sound healing, meditation, and breathwork, Adam is a 500HR+ ERYT. His journey into yoga began during a challenging period in his life, leading him to discover the transformative power of these practices.

Adam is passionate about making yoga accessible to everyone, regardless of their background or experience level. His mission is to empower individuals through mindful movement, breath awareness, and the healing vibrations of sound, fostering a nurturing and uplifting environment for all participants. Through his teachings, Adam aims to create a holistic experience that promotes physical, mental, and emotional well-being, helping others to navigate life’s challenges with resilience and grace.

#### Julia

@awildbreathwork

[Link] "@awildbreathwork" → https://www.instagram.com/awildbreathwork/#

Julia Aurora is a Scandinavian ceremonialist, sound therapist, yoga & meditation & breathwork focused on neurocognitive function and nervous system rewire. Rooted in somatics and feminine nature, she guides deep embodiment through breath, sound, somatic movement, and art therapy. Weaving earth’s wisdom with the ethereal frequencies of light language, she invites a homecoming, where intuition awakens and the soul remembers its song.

Julia carries the teachings of shamanicas, offering a space where music becomes medicine channeled from water and trees. Through breath and sacred sound, she alchemizes pain into pleasure, tension into flow, and limitation into liberation. Her classes blend handpan, crystal bowls, shamanic drums, chacapa, and nature seeds—each carrying the voices of the elements, harmonizing body, spirit, and breath into one rhythm.
