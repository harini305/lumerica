#### About Weaving

Weaving is an essential aspect of Balinese culture, deeply embedded in the island’s traditions and daily life. Utilizing native plants like the lontar palm, Balinese weavers craft intricate baskets and other items that are both functional and symbolic. Each woven piece represents the island’s rich heritage and the skillful artistry passed down through generations. The practice of weaving is not only a means of creating beautiful and practical objects but also serves as a communal activity, strengthening social bonds and preserving cultural traditions. Through weaving, the Balinese people maintain a connection to their ancestors, celebrate their identity, and sustain their cultural legacy.
