## Section 2

Step into stillness of beingness, a deeply nourishing immersion where ancient mythic storytelling, yin yoga, and sound medicine intertwine to cradle body, mind, and spirit.

This is a space to soften.

A space to listen beyond the thinking mind.

Through long, supported shapes, the body gently unwinds, inviting the fascia and muscles to release layers of held tension. Guided imagery and evocative storytelling open an inner landscape for reflection, while waves of sound healing carry you into a state of profound rest. An extended shavasana with channeled soundhealing becomes a quiet presence, allowing the nervous system to shift from striving into surrender.

Here, you are invited to slow down, to drift inward, and to reconnect with the subtle rhythms of your own being, emerging grounded, restored, and deeply held.

Session includes:

~ Guided arrival with breath awareness and storytelling

~ 1.5 hours of slow, mindful Yin practice to release deep tension

~ Long supported savasana with reiki and sound

~ Immersive sound healing journey for integration

Participants may experience deep relaxation, emotional release, improved sleep quality, and a profound sense of calm and inner spaciousness. Participants leave feeling grounded, restored, and gently reconnected to their inner rhythm.
