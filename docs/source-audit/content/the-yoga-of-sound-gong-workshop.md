## Section 2

The vibration of Gong creates deep relaxation, stimulates glandular and nervous systems to higher levels of functioning. It releases blocks, reduces tension and rearranges our emotional energy. Gong Sound Healing is an integrated element of Kundalini Yoga technology which amplifies, projects and elevates energy circulation to the areas of body and mind that were not accessible before, leading to rejuvenation, rebirth, deep self-awareness.

In this workshop we will look at the importance of Gong sound as a healing tool. Firstly, we will concentrate on ancient and contemporary knowledge on the Gong sound therapeutics. Then, we will go through a gentle sequence of breathing, yoga and meditation exercises to prepare ourselves for a deeply penetrating healing through the sound of the Gong. Lastly, we will focus on practical details of the art of gong-playing followed by individual hands-on sessions.

This is an incredible workshop for new and regular Gong practitioners and particularly suitable for those new to Gong and want to explore the scientific, spiritual, practice and play of the Gong. Everyone is welcome!
